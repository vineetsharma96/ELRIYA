# ELYRIA — Vision

Elyria is a cute, living, futuristic 3D open-world city running in the browser.

## Experience

The player controls a cute stylized protagonist and freely explores a large city containing:
- Cute NPC citizens
- Vehicles and traffic
- Enterable buildings
- Cozy interiors
- Parks and waterways
- Trees, flowers and falling petals
- Dynamic weather
- Wind simulation
- Sun/moon cycle
- Interactive map and navigation

The world should feel peaceful, alive, colorful and explorable.

## Art Direction

Use the provided Elyria concept image as the visual north star.

Target:
- Cute stylized 3D
- Chibi characters
- Rounded architecture
- Pastel colors
- Lush vegetation
- Cherry blossoms
- Cozy interiors
- Bright futuristic technology
- Clean skies and atmospheric depth
- Beautiful sunset/night environments

Avoid:
- Dark cyberpunk
- Dystopian environments
- Generic realistic city
- Excessive neon
- Empty environments

The goal:

> "I want to live in this world."
