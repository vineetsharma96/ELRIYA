# ELYRIA visual and interaction design

The intended feeling is cozy, cute, futuristic and alive. Use original chibi silhouettes, soft corners, pastel facades, lush planting, warm cafe light and clean skies. The reference PNG named in `Prompt.md` is missing at initial inspection. All palette and composition decisions below are provisional interpretations of the written brief, not claims of reference matching.

## M1 visual benchmark

One walkable plaza in Blossom Central should communicate a complete small place: cafe frontage and seating, a compact apartment, rounded futuristic skyline accent, water with a bridge or waterside path, blossom trees, flowers, drifting petals, a protagonist, companion, a few citizens and a cute vehicle.

Compose a clear pedestrian route and a recognizable cafe entrance. Separate the foreground characters, middle-distance water/trees and background architecture with value and atmospheric depth. Preserve open walking space and readable silhouettes; density should come from purposeful groupings, not scattered objects.

Provisional palette: warm cream and apricot facades, teal water/technology, soft coral signs, blossom pink, sage foliage and restrained indigo night accents. Reserve saturated accents for destinations and small storytelling details. Night retains visible streets and welcoming warm windows.

## Interaction baseline

Desktop target: WASD movement, mouse camera, Shift sprint, Space jump, E interact, M map, C camera mode, Escape menu. Only advertise bindings when implemented. Provide visible touch movement/camera/action inputs when mobile support is tested. Maintain usable focus behavior; avoid trapping keyboard input in dialogs.

M0 can use an explicitly provisional character/controller. M1 must show an original cute protagonist asset. Full skeletal animation, blend transitions, slopes, stairs and robust camera obstruction handling are M2 requirements and must not be claimed merely because a character mesh moves.

Companion and citizens initially establish scale and personality. Full following behavior, schedules, dialogue and traffic are separately gated systems. A vehicle parked in the scene is a visual asset, not an implemented traffic simulation.

## Acceptance review

- Inspect the supplied concept once available and record observed composition, materials, lighting, character proportions and vegetation relationships.
- Capture the actual runtime at a pedestrian viewpoint and a wider composition, in daylight and at night when the time system exists.
- Confirm character readability, inviting cafe frontage, coherent scale, original asset provenance and unobstructed movement routes.
- Check materials and contours under real runtime lighting; a Blender viewport alone is insufficient.
- Verify no broken assets, severe aliasing, clipping, uncontrolled bloom, hidden quality failures or fake feature controls.
- Evaluate the same scene against measured desktop and mobile budgets. Record hardware, browser, viewport and quality.

Do not expand to an entire district until the reference review and plaza acceptance are complete.
